Improvements for the client this server serves; nothing changes on the server itself.

## Before you update

- **Nothing to do:** no database migration, no new variable. `squorli update` is enough.
- **A new desktop app (0.7.1) follows after this server.** Older apps keep working; the changes below reach their users only with the new app, and browser users with this update.
- **Reporting a direct message needs a current Squorli Directory** (26 September 2026 or newer). With an older directory the flag on direct messages is simply not shown.

## New

- **Report a direct message.** The flag on a friend's message reports it to the operator of the directory, not to your server: direct messages are end-to-end encrypted, so the reporting person's client sends the message and, unless they untick it, up to 20 preceding messages in plain text with the report. The reported person does not learn who reported. Afterwards the dialog offers to block that friend.
- **A clear notice when a server is down.** A server you are signed in to that does not answer no longer shows the login: the client keeps your session, shows a notice with your other reachable servers, tries again by itself (after 15 seconds, then 30 seconds, then every minute, ten times in all) and then waits for "Try again". Sign-out stays possible from there.

## Fixed

- **Screen share audio on a separate output device** no longer moves everybody's voices to that device as well (a limitation of Chromium-based browsers the client now works around). The share's volume, deafen and "not listening" still apply to it.
- **A share you watch plays its audio at once**, without pinning it or showing it in fullscreen.
- **A server that does not answer at all** counts as unreachable after a few seconds instead of leaving the client waiting.
