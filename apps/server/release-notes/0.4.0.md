A check that tells you what is wrong with a setup, and reports for your moderators.

## Before you update

- **Back up first** (`squorli backup`): the update adds the tables for reports and the moderation log and gives the new right "Handle reports" to every role that may delete other people's messages. It runs at the start and cannot be undone.
- **Run the installer again** (`sudo bash install.sh`, choose "Update") if you want `squorli doctor` on the command line: it is part of the `squorli` command the installer writes. `squorli update` alone pulls the image and does not touch that command.
- **Two optional variables** for installations that changed the media ports by hand: `LIVEKIT_TCP_PORT` and `LIVEKIT_UDP_PORT` (defaults 7881 and 7882) are now also read by the app so the check names the right ports. The current `deploy/compose.yml` passes them; installations with an older Compose file, and Portainer stacks, only need to set them if their ports differ.
- **The check from outside needs the current Squorli Directory** (26 September 2026 or newer). With an older directory the check still runs, only the outside view is missing.
- **A new desktop app follows after this server.** Older apps keep working; the report menus, the report queue and the connection check appear only in the new app and in the browser client of this server.

## New

- **Connection check.** Administration > Server > "Check the connection" tests what goes wrong most often in a setup and names the likely cause: domain and certificate, the WebSocket upgrade and `/rtc` through your proxy, LiveKit and its key, the media ports, the trusted proxies, the directory registration. Your browser also makes a real media connection and tells you whether it runs over UDP, over TCP only, or not at all, and whether LiveKit announces a private address. The same check on the server: `squorli doctor`.
- **Reports.** Members can report a message or a member to your moderators (the flag on a message, "Report" in the member menu). The report keeps a copy of what was reported, attachments included, so it stays reviewable after the message is gone. Administration > Reports shows the queue with the actions: delete the message, delete the member's messages of the last hour, day or week, kick, ban, or close without action. Open reports show as a badge on the server rail. The reported person never learns who reported; when a moderator deletes something of theirs they get a short notice. Copies are removed 30 days after a report is closed, 90 days after it was made at the latest.
- **The right "Handle reports"** in the role editor under Members. Every role that may delete other people's messages gets it with the update; owners and administrators always have it.
- **Moderation log.** Administration > Reports also lists who deleted somebody else's message, kicked, banned, unbanned, blocked a channel or closed a report, with when and whom, never with message contents. Kept for 180 days.
- **Delete messages on ban.** Banning a member now asks whether their messages of the last hour, 24 hours or 7 days should go with them.
