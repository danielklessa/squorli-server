## New

- **A sound for new direct messages and mentions.** It stays off while you have that conversation in front of you. Switch it under Settings > Sounds > Messages.
- **The task bar icon shows what is waiting.** A number on the icon counts unread direct messages and mentions, and the tray icon gets a dot.
- **Start with the system.** Settings > View > System start. You choose whether Squorli then starts in the background (in the tray, or minimized) or opens its window. Off by default.
- **YouTube playlists as a radio source.** Paste a playlist address and the channel plays it as a queue for everyone. Members who may control the radio can skip back and forth. Needs a server that is up to date.
- **Twitch and YouTube follow the radio's output device.** The players' sound now goes to the device chosen for the web radio instead of always using the system's default.

## Improved

- When your radio is off and a Twitch or YouTube video is running in the channel, a tile in the voice view tells you and lets you turn the radio back on. You can dismiss it; it returns with the next video.
- An x on the embedded player turns the radio off for you, next to "open in a window of its own".
- YouTube videos start without subtitles. The player's CC button still turns them on.
- Microphone and camera handling from the recent mobile work: the camera chosen in the dialog is the one that opens, and a changed send resolution applies to a running camera.

## Fixed

- The embedded video no longer sticks out over the rounded corners of its tile.
- A stereo microphone that delivers its signal on one channel only is heard on both sides.
