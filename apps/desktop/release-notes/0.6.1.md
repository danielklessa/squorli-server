## Improved

- **Your keys are encrypted by the system.** Your identity key and the keys of your server accounts no longer lie readable in the app's storage: Windows keeps them encrypted for your user account (on Linux the desktop's key ring, where there is one; without one they stay as before). They move over by themselves at the first start. After this update, do not go back to an older app: it would no longer find your key (with a directory account, signing in with your password brings it back).
- **The camera and screen permission of a voice channel now counts.** When a channel keeps camera and screen from a role or a member, your app no longer sends yours to them. Needs a server with version 0.2.0 or newer.
- **Creating a server account as a member from before** now gives you a fresh key for that server; your main key stays on your computer. Your messages and roles move with you. Needs a server with version 0.2.0 or newer.
- **Server account passwords are bound to their server**, so a password you also use elsewhere is worth nothing to another server. Your account switches over at its next sign-in with the password.
- **Servers from your list that this computer has never been on** show in the server bar but connect only when you click them.
- When a server asks you to slow down, you see a short "please wait" instead of an error code.
- Pictures in a channel you keep open for days keep loading: the app fetches fresh links for them by itself.

## Fixed

- **A server could sign you in to another server.** A server you added could claim to be a different one and pass your sign-in on. The app now signs only for the address it actually connects to, and refuses a server that names another one.
