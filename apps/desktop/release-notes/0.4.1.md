## New

- **See who is muted, deafened, on camera or sharing in every voice channel.** The microphone, headphone, camera and screen icons next to a person's name in the channel list now show for every voice channel, not only the one you are in. Needs a server that is up to date; on older servers the icons still appear only inside your own channel.
- **Status API for your server.** Administrators find a new section under Administration > Server: the server can publish its channels and who is where (including muted, deafened, camera and screen) as JSON, for a widget on your website or a stream overlay. It is off by default and can require a key or be public. Needs a server that is up to date.
- **squorli:// links are clickable in messages.** A `squorli://server/…` or `squorli://invite/…` link in a chat or direct message opens the server right in the app. Command links (`squorli://control/…`) are never turned into links.
- **Invitations as app links.** Under Administration > Invitations every invitation has "Copy app link" next to "Copy link": the app link opens the invitation directly in the desktop app.
- **Paste files into the chat.** Ctrl+V or the context menu's "Paste" puts a picture from the clipboard or files copied in the file manager into the message's attachments; pictures show a small preview.
- **Right-click menu in the window.** Cut, copy, paste, undo and select all in text fields, spelling suggestions and "add to dictionary", copy or open a link, copy or save a picture. The menu follows the app's language.

## Improved

- Every copy button answers: "Copied" with a check for a moment, or "Could not copy" when the clipboard refuses.
