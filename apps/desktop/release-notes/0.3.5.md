## New

- **Statistics for a screen share you are watching.** Move the mouse over the share's tile in the voice view, or over its own window, and press the activity button. A small box in the corner shows how the picture arrives: codec and decoder, picture size, frames per second, bandwidth, packet loss, delay, and how often the picture froze. A last line says where a stutter most likely comes from: the network, your own computer, or the sender. Press the button again to hide it.
