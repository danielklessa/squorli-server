## New

- **Report a message or a member.** The flag next to a message and "Report" in a member's menu send a report to that server's moderators, with a reason and a short note. The moderators get a copy of what you reported; the reported person does not learn who reported. Needs a server on version 0.4.0 or newer; on older servers the flag is not shown.
- **Reports for moderators.** Whoever holds the new right "Handle reports" finds the queue under Administration > Reports: each report with a copy of the message, and the actions delete, delete the member's recent messages, kick, ban, or close without action. Open reports show as a badge on the server in the rail. Below the queue, the moderation log lists who kicked, banned, blocked or deleted what, without message contents. Needs server 0.4.0.
- **Connection check.** Administration > Server > "Check the connection" shows the server's own diagnosis of its setup (domain, certificate, proxy, LiveKit, media ports, directory) and makes a real media connection from your computer, telling you whether it runs over UDP, over TCP only, or not at all. For administrators; needs server 0.4.0.
- **Delete messages when banning.** Banning a member now asks whether their messages of the last hour, 24 hours or 7 days should go too. Needs server 0.4.0.

## Improved

- **A notice when a moderator removes something of yours** after a report, naming the channel but not who reported.
