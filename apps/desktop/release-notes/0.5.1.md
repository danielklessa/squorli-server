## New

- **Remove and block people from voice channels.** In a person's menu, "Remove from the voice channel" takes them out of the channel; its submenu also keeps them out of it for 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes or for good. Moderators see a person's channel blocks in the same menu and can lift them there, or under channel dialog > Blocks. A passed vote kick now shows up as such a block too. Needs "Move members" in that channel and a server that is up to date.
- **One menu for voice members.** Right-clicking a person in a voice channel in the channel list and right-clicking their tile on the stage now open the same menu: volume, turning their camera or screen off or on for yourself, move, remove and block, end their camera and screen, block streaming, lift blocks and the vote kick.
- **Small profile.** A left click on a member opens a small profile: for a friend a line to write them a message straight away, otherwise a button to send a friend request. The full menu stays on the right click.
- **Remove and block friends.** Right-click a friend in the friends view, use the dots button at the top of a conversation, or choose "Remove friend" in the member list's menu. Each asks first.
- **Voice activity on the server rail.** A small green speaker on a server's icon shows that people sit in its voice channels; the tooltip says how many.

## Improved

- The member list's menu is tidier: roles, owner status, kick and ban sit together under "Server"; the volume slider is left to the voice menus.
- "End camera/screen" is greyed out while the person shares neither.
- Roles are listed in the server's own order everywhere.
- Menus open on a right click only.

## Fixed

- Global shortcuts follow your keyboard layout: on a German keyboard Ctrl+Ö no longer registers Ctrl+Ü, and Ctrl+Y no longer Ctrl+Z. The settings show the key as your keyboard labels it. Windows only.
- After joining a voice channel once, clicking another voice channel showed the first text channel instead of the stage.
- Someone removed from a voice channel saw "Disconnected by the server (PARTICIPANT_REMOVED)"; they now get a notice that says who removed them and for how long the channel is closed to them. Needs a server that is up to date.
