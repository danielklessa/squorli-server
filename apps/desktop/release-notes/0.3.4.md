## New

- **H.265 for game shares, encoded by the graphics card.** Where your computer can send H.265, Quick Share uses it, and the share dialog offers it in place of H.264 and preselects it for a detected game's window. The graphics card does the encoding instead of the processor, which leaves more room for the game, and the same picture needs less bandwidth. Checked with an NVIDIA card so far; computers that cannot send H.265 keep H.264. If someone watches who cannot play H.265 (Firefox, for example), the share switches to VP8 for everyone. Windows only, like the game detection itself.

## Improved

- **Game shares are tuned for motion.** A share sent with H.264 or H.265 now keeps its frame rate when things get tight and gives up some sharpness instead, and it may use more bandwidth than before (up to 8 Mbit/s with H.264, 6 Mbit/s with H.265). Shares with VP8 stay tuned for sharp text, as before.

## Fixed

- Sharing a game with Quick Share or with H.264 stuttered and showed block artifacts: the picture was encoded at about half the intended frame rate. It now runs at the full 30 frames per second.
