## New

- **Link previews.** A link in a channel message now shows a card with the page's name, title, a short description and a picture. Your server fetches these and hands you the picture itself, so the linked site never sees who reads the message. Whoever wrote the message can take a preview away with the x next to it, and a link written in angle brackets, `<https://…>`, is sent without one. Needs a server that is up to date.
- **YouTube videos play right in the chat.** A YouTube link becomes a card with the video's title, its picture and a play button. The player is only loaded when you press play; until then nothing is sent to YouTube. The author can remove it with the x like any other preview.
- **Previews in direct messages, too.** Direct messages stay end-to-end encrypted: the preview is made on your computer before the message is encrypted. The app asks the linked site itself, so no server learns which link you sent, and the picture is stored encrypted, only you and your friend can open it. The x removes a preview for both of you; a friend who still uses an older version of the app sees an empty message instead. Needs a directory that is up to date.

## Improved

- **The sound of a video in the chat follows your screen audio device.** It plays on the device chosen under Settings > Voice and audio > Output > "Screen audio" (or where voices play if none is chosen), a few seconds after the video starts. Web radio players keep their own device. Checked on Windows.
