## New

- **Report a direct message.** The flag on a friend's message reports it to the operator of the directory, not to a server: direct messages are end-to-end encrypted, so your app sends the message and, unless you untick it, up to 20 preceding messages in plain text with the report. The reported person does not learn who reported. Afterwards the dialog offers to block that friend. Needs a Squorli Directory that takes reports (26 September 2026 or newer); otherwise the flag is not shown.
- **A clear notice when a server is down.** A server you are signed in to that does not answer no longer shows the login: the app keeps your session, shows a notice with your other reachable servers, tries again by itself (after 15 seconds, then 30 seconds, then every minute, ten times in all) and then waits for "Try again". Sign-out stays possible from there.

## Fixed

- **Screen share audio on a separate output device** no longer moves everybody's voices to that device as well. The share's volume, deafen and "not listening" still apply to it.
- **A share you watch plays its audio at once**, without pinning it or showing it in fullscreen.
- **A server that does not answer at all** counts as unreachable after a few seconds instead of leaving the app waiting; the app starts into the notice instead of an empty window.
