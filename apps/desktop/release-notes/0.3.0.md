## New

- **Show which game you are playing.** Off until you turn it on under Settings > Games. Your friends then see "Playing …" with the game's icon next to your name, and so do the members of your servers unless you untick "Show it to the members of my servers too". Squorli knows the games that Steam, the Epic Games Launcher and the Xbox app have installed (GOG too, which has seen the least testing so far), and you can add other programs by hand. A game without a tick in the list is never shown to anybody, and the list can be set before a game's first start. Only the running game is ever sent, never the list of your games; programs that are no games, such as Wallpaper Engine, are left out by themselves. The switch belongs to your account, the list to this computer. Detecting games works on Windows; seeing what others play works everywhere. Needs a server that is up to date.
- **Playing with a controller no longer makes you "away".** Input from gamepads, joysticks and other game controllers now counts as activity like keyboard and mouse, also while Squorli is in the background, so a controller game no longer moves you to the AFK channel. Windows only.
- **Watching a video no longer makes you "away".** While a video plays on your computer, in a browser or a player, in fullscreen or not, you stay present for up to four hours after your last input. Squorli only learns that the screen is being kept on, not what you watch, and that stays on your computer. Windows only.
