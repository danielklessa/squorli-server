## Improved

- **Control links are protected.** The links under Settings > Hotkeys that switch the microphone on, or toggle microphone or sound, now carry a key of your installation, so no web page can open your microphone through them. **Links set up before this version for those actions stop working: copy them again** from Settings > Hotkeys into your Stream Deck, G Hub or macro tool. Links that only mute you or switch the sound off, and the command line, work as before.
- **A new password signs your server account out on your other devices.**
- **Microphone boost is off for new devices.** Switch it on under Settings > Voice and audio if your microphone is too quiet; a computer that already stored its settings keeps them.

## Fixed

- **Links from servers and other members** open only when they are ordinary web links.
- **The app signs for the directory only at the address it actually connects to**, and refuses a directory that claims to be another one.
