## New

- **Permissions per channel and category.** Right-click a channel or a category heading and choose "Edit channel" or "Edit category": who may see, enter, write, attach files, share camera or screen, control the radio, move or moderate can be allowed or denied per role or per member, in a simple or an advanced view. A category's settings apply to its channels; a channel can deviate. A channel you may not see no longer appears at all, not even its name. Needs a server that is up to date; on older servers the menu entries are not offered.
- **Private and read-only channels.** Two switches in the channel dialog. A private channel carries a lock; a private voice channel shows the lock in place of its speaker.
- **Sticky voice channels.** Whoever sits in one cannot change to another voice channel until somebody with "Move members" moves them out; the channel carries a pin, and the hang-up button says why it is disabled. Per channel you can also choose whether the hold survives a reload and whether the other voice channels are hidden meanwhile. Administrators can exempt roles or members with "Not held by sticky voice channels".
- **User limit, slow mode and a notification suggestion per channel.** A limited voice channel shows "3/5" in the channel list; in a slow-mode text channel the send button counts the wait down; a channel can suggest "all", "mentions only" or "nothing" (your own mute still wins). Web radio and camera/screen can be switched off for a channel.
- **Vote kick in voice channels.** When at least three people sit in a voice channel and none of them may remove members, "Start vote kick" appears in a person's menu. The vote runs for a minute: the starter counts as a yes, the person it is about cannot vote, everybody else gets the question. A tie keeps the person, and at least half of the channel has to vote for the result to count. A passed vote removes the person from the channel and keeps them out of it for 15 minutes. The running tally, with the votes as a bar, sits at the top of the member list. Administrators can switch it off per channel under channel dialog > Voice. Needs a server that is up to date.
- **"Move members" is a permission of its own.** Moving somebody to another voice channel (menu or drag and drop) no longer comes with "Moderate voice channels"; roles that could moderate before got it automatically.

## Improved

- The settings, administration and channel dialogs are wider on large windows, keep long labels readable and no longer let texts overlap; categories stay on the left.
- Administration > Channels is an overview now (create, sort, delete, AFK channel); every row has a pencil that opens the channel dialog for name, topic, category and everything else.
- A refused join or move says why in a sentence ("You may not enter this channel", "The channel is full", …) instead of showing an error code.
- A voice channel you may not enter is dimmed in the channel list, with the reason as its tooltip.
- Importing a Discord template takes the template's channel permissions over, so imported private channels stay private.
- The Status API section under Administration > Server describes what the server publishes: only the people who sit in a voice channel.

## Fixed

- Reloading while a sticky channel holds you no longer ends with "Client initiated disconnect": you are back in the channel with a short notice.
- A role just added in the permissions editor no longer vanishes before its first switch is set.
