## New

- **One voice connection per account.** Joining a voice channel from a second device or tab while you are already in one on that server now asks first, because it ends the other connection. The other device hangs up and tells you why. Needs a server that is up to date; an older server keeps its old behaviour.

## Improved

- Notices and errors about your voice connection (a moderator moved or removed you, a lost connection, a camera that did not start) open as a small dialog with a close button instead of a line in the bottom left corner. Clicking beside the dialog or pressing Escape closes it too.
- When another device took over your voice channel, the message says so in plain words.
