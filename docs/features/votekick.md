**Vote kick (23 September 2026; user's wish: "wenn in einem Sprachkanal mindestens 3 Benutzer sind und keiner davon hat das Recht in diesem Raum Benutzer zu kicken soll es die Möglichkeit geben einen Votekick gegen einen Benutzer zu starten. Im Context Menü auf dem Benutzer ist dafür 'Vote Kick' auswählbar, wenn ein Vote läuft ist das aktuelle Ergebnis für alle im Raum rechts über der Benutzerliste sichtbar. Derjenige der ihn startet zählt als 'ja', derjenige, gegen den gevotet wird darf nicht mitabstimmen, alle anderen bekommen ein Modal in dem sie 'Ja' oder 'Nein' wählen können. Der vote läuft für eine Minute, bei einem Unentschieden bleibt der Benutzer, läuft die Zeit ab muss mindestens die hälfte aller im Rum befindlichen Benutzer abgestimmt haben damit das voting zählt. Das Ergebnis wird rechts oben mitgeteilt und verschwindet nach 30 Sekunden wieder."):**

The user's decisions, asked before the work began:

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| What a passed vote does | Out of the voice channel **and** locked out of that one channel for 15 minutes (`VOTEKICK_BLOCK_MS`); every other channel stays open. Without a lock-out the member would simply rejoin |
| An admin's switch | Per channel in the channel dialog ("Vote-Kick in diesem Kanal erlauben", `Channel.allowVoteKick`), on by default |
| The quorum | Half of **everybody who sat in the channel**, the member the vote is about included: three in the room need two votes (`voteKickQuorum` = `ceil(roomSize / 2)`) |
| Who may vote | Fixed when the vote starts: whoever sits in the channel then, except the member it is about. Joining later gives no vote and changes no quorum; leaving counts as not having voted |

**When a vote is possible (the user's rule):** the channel allows it, at least three members sit in it (`VOTEKICK_MIN_MEMBERS`), no vote is running, and **nobody present may throw anybody out by themselves**. "May throw out" = `MOVE_MEMBERS` resolved in that channel (that is what removes somebody from a voice channel, `docs/features/channel-permissions.md`) or `KICK_MEMBERS` server-wide; owners and administrators hold both, so a moderator in the room switches the feature off for as long as they are there. The server answers this per channel in `voice.state` (`voteKick: boolean`, `voiceStateEvent`), because a client cannot resolve another member's permissions in a channel; the clients offer the menu entry exactly then, and the start route checks everything again.

**The course of a vote:** `POST /api/channels/:id/votekick { targetId }` starts it, the starter's yes is already in it (user's rule). Everybody in the channel gets the event `votekick` with the counts, the end time and, per recipient, their own vote and whether they may still vote; whoever may vote and has not yet also gets the modal. `POST /api/channels/:id/votekick/vote { yes }` casts one vote, once, without changing it. The vote ends after a minute, or as soon as everybody who may vote has voted (nothing left to wait for), or at once when the member it is about leaves the channel (`cancelled`, decides nothing). Then everybody in the channel gets `votekick.result`: `quorum` (too few votes), `tie` (the member stays, user's rule), `rejected`, `passed`. The clients show it for 30 seconds (`VOTEKICK_RESULT_MS`, the client's own timer).

**A passed vote:** the member gets the result first and then `voice.moved { channelId: null }`, so their client can say why it is hanging up; the server drops their seat (`VoicePresence.leaveUser`) and removes the participant at LiveKit, whatever the client does. `VoteKicks.block` then keeps them out of that channel: `POST /api/rtc-token` answers `403 votekicked` with `until`, `voice.join` answers `forbidden`. A moderator's move (`MoveGrants`) wins over the block, so somebody with `MOVE_MEMBERS` can always put them back. A kick or ban clears blocks and cooldowns (`clearUser`), a deleted channel takes its own with it (`clearChannel`).

**Claude's decisions, not asked:** a cooldown of 5 minutes for a new vote about the same member in the same channel after one ended (`VOTEKICK_COOLDOWN_MS`, `429 cooldown`; without it a majority could vote in a loop); one vote per channel at a time; the counts travel, never who voted how (these three confirmed by the user on 25 September 2026); the starter leaving does not end the vote (their yes stands, they are in the roster the quorum counts over); closing the modal is no vote, the box keeps both buttons until the minute is over; the result box is the client's own timer, so a reload inside those 30 seconds loses it.

**In memory, single node** (like `VoicePresence` and `MoveGrants`): a restart forgets running votes and cooldowns. The members simply vote again. **Since 24 September 2026 the 15-minute block is a channel block** (`docs/features/channel-blocks.md`, table `channel_blocks`, `source: "votekick"`): it survives a restart, and a moderator sees and lifts it in the member menu or the channel dialog.

**Protocol:** no `PROTOCOL_VERSION` bump. `votekick` and `votekick.result` are new events (a client from before drops what it cannot parse), `voice.state.voteKick` and `Channel.allowVoteKick` have defaults (`false` resp. `true`), and starting and voting are REST, not client events: an older server answers 404 instead of `bad_message`, and the refusals can say why (`apiErrorText.ts` turns `votekick_disabled`, `moderator_present`, `too_few_members`, `not_in_channel`, `target_not_in_channel`, `vote_running`, `cooldown`, `no_vote`, `not_eligible`, `already_voted` into sentences).

**Client:** the menu entry "Vote-Kick starten" sits in the member list, on a voice member in the sidebar and on a tile of the stage; it appears only when the server offers a vote in the channel and both of us sit in it (`voteKickChannel`). The box (`VoteKickPanel`) is the first element **inside** the member list (user's correction of 23 September 2026: "der Vote kick soll nicht über der Liste sein, sondern oben in der Liste"), rendered by `MemberList` through its `voteKickBox` prop and scrolling with the list; a coloured left edge says what it is (brand while it runs, coral for `passed`, mint when the member stays). It shows the countdown, the member the vote is about, the bar, the counts, how many votes are still missing for the quorum, and the two buttons for whoever may still vote. `App.tsx` hands it over only while the member list belongs to the server the voice connection is on.

**The member the vote is about is drawn like members are drawn everywhere else** (user's wish of 23 September 2026): avatar or initials with the presence dot and the name in the colour of their highest role (`voteKickPerson`, `topRoleOf` in `memberRank.ts`, which the member list now uses too). Somebody who is no longer a member keeps the name the vote carries, without avatar or colour. Because the row names them, the result sentences do not repeat the name ("Wurde aus dem Kanal gewählt.").

**Yes and no are a bar** (user's wish: "das ja nein bitte als Balken optisch darstellen"): its full width is everybody who sat in the channel when the vote started, yes grows from the left in coral (the member goes), no follows in mint (they stay), the rest is what has not voted, and a thin mark sits where the quorum is reached (`voteBar`). The counts stand under it in the same two colours.

**Consequence of the placement:** where the member list is not shown, the box is not either — below 900 px window width, on a phone until the member button opens the list, and in the home view. The question modal still reaches everybody who may vote, so nobody misses a vote; only the running tally needs the list.

The look in headless Chrome over the DevTools protocol against the built CSS: first the floating box at 1440, 900, 390 and 320 px (no horizontal overflow at any width, buttons side by side down to 320 px), after the user's two corrections the box inside a member column of 15 rem next to real member rows, both result variants and the modal; the bar, the quorum mark, the person row with the role colour and the truncation of a long name looked at.
