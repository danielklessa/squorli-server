Simply Stay Consistent

Blue for actions, dark surfaces for calm conversation spaces. Mint, Amber and Coral signal status and are always combined with text or symbols. No additional gradients in the logo.

Use the normal icon mark from 32 px upwards, the eyeless small variant for 16–24 px. Clear space: at least one eighth of the icon mark's width. UI font: Segoe UI with system-ui as fallback, body text 16 px and line height 1.5.
