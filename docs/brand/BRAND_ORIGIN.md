# Squorli — Name Origin and Brand Concept

## Background

The name **Squorli** was developed on September 14, 2026, for an internationally oriented chat and voice platform serving gaming and creator communities. It was selected from ten proposed names.

## Name Formation

Squorli is a coined name. The following words inspired its creation; they represent creative associations rather than a linguistic etymology:

- **Squad:** A group of friends or teammates. The opening “Squ” connects the name to community and playing together.
- **Swirl:** A circular movement, suggesting lively conversations and ideas moving between people.
- **Circle:** A circle of friends, representing belonging and a shared meeting place.
- **The ending “li”:** Chosen for its soft, playful sound. It has no independent literal meaning here.

The intended pronunciation is approximately **“SKWOR-lee,”** with emphasis on the first syllable.

## Brand Meaning

Squorli represents a lively circle of friends who stay connected through conversation: a digital place to gather, play, create, and share.

The name is intended to feel friendly, memorable, and easy to pronounce internationally. It suggests gaming without limiting the brand exclusively to games.

## Connection to the Logo

The three circling speech characters translate the name’s concept into a visual identity:

- The **speech characters** represent people, voices, and conversation.
- Their **circular arrangement** reflects the associations with “swirl” and “circle.”
- The **three individual characters** express community.
- Their **full, rounded shapes** give the brand a friendly, playful personality.

The preferred identity uses a light blue symbol on a dark navy background with a light wordmark. Dark mode is the default design direction.

## Short Brand Statement

**Squorli combines the togetherness of a squad, the movement of a swirl, and the belonging of a circle of friends—a shared place for voices, games, and conversations.**

## Documentation Scope

This document records the intended reasoning behind the name. It does not establish worldwide first use, trademark eligibility, or continued availability of domains and social media handles.
